Why it matters

Most crew profiles were written once, years ago, and never updated, or copied word for word from the CV. The first thing an experienced recruiter compares is consistency: titles, vessels, dates and licences. A profile that contradicts the CV raises a question you are not there to answer; one that matches it reinforces your application.

What you receive

  • HeadlineThree options stating your main licence, current role and target position.
  • About150 to 250 words, in the first person, in short paragraphs, written for the phone.
  • ExperienceOne entry per position: exact title, vessel and dates from your CV, a line of vessel details, two or three bullet points.
  • Licences and skillsYour main licences with issuing body, number and validity; ten to fifteen skills from your competencies.
  • Consistency table and guideEvery key fact of your CV compared with LinkedIn, and where to paste each section.
