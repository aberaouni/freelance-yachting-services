Why it matters

Every attractive opening draws a stack of applications, and captains screen between watches. CVs are inflated, certificates are listed without dates and timelines are vague. CrewCheck gives you a second, expert read before a name goes on the shortlist.

What you receive

  • Credentials and certificatesPresence, dating and coherence of STCW, CoC/MCA, ENG1 and vessel-specific tickets.
  • Sea experienceVessel types and sizes, roles and itineraries, read with an industry eye.
  • Career consistencyTimeline continuity, gaps between contracts and job-hop patterns.
  • Red flagsInflated titles, undated or missing documents, timeline holes.
  • The verdictShortlist, verify further, or pass.

Our rules

  • The report stays with you: never shared with the candidate.
  • Screening is document-based: no interview.
  • Reference and background checks are not included.
  • A full CV rewrite is the candidate’s own CV Audit.
