MLC

Never pay to be placed.

Under the Maritime Labour Convention 2006, recruitment and placement services must not charge seafarers for finding them work. If an agency asks you to pay for a placement, walk away.

Before you write to an agency

Five habits that get you placed.

  1. Choose three to five agenciesThe established agencies of your hub. Register properly with each.
  2. Be in the right portSpring in Antibes and Palma, autumn in Fort Lauderdale.
  3. Keep your profile currentDates, certificates and availability, every time something changes.
  4. Show upWhen you are in port, call or visit. Recruiters remember faces.
  5. Send a CV that reads wellIt is your first interview with the agency, and its first argument with the captain.
